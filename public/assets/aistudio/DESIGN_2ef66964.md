## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a gentle, comforting, and affectionate visual identity tailored for early childhood spaces, picture books, bedtime rituals, and secure attachment toys. It evokes the tender reassurance of golden morning sun filtering through nursery curtains paired with the soothing calm of evening lavender. 

The aesthetic is Tactile Soft-Minimalism: warm, rounded, pillowy, and physically inviting without falling into cluttered nursery tropes. Visual metaphors prioritize softness over sharpness, tactile depth over sterile flatness, and restful breathing room over sensory overload. It appeals directly to mindful caregivers seeking trust, calm, and nostalgia, as well as toddlers navigating intuitive, tactile micro-interactions.

## Colors

The palette revolves around four foundational tones that convey emotional warmth, gentle balance, and security:

- **Primary (`#E9A824` - Toasted Honey):** A radiant, golden nectar used for primary interactions, key touchpoints, and focal celebrations. It channels energetic warmth, optimism, and daylight play.
- **Secondary (`#8E7DBE` - Sweet Lavender):** A soothing, soft dusk purple that provides grounding contrast. Applied to navigational landmarks, secondary highlights, bedtime modes, and reflective moments.
- **Tertiary (`#F4ACB7` - Powder Rose):** A tender, comforting petal pink that supports micro-delights, badges, warm tags, and empathetic alerts.
- **Neutral Surface (`#FFFDF9` - Warm Ivory):** A restorative, dairy-warm canvas that avoids the harsh clinical glare of pure white.

### Functional Roles & Contrast
- **Typography & Dark Contrast:** Use a deep cocoa-plum neutral (`#3D323E`) for high-legibility copy instead of pure charcoal, preserving chromatic harmony. Muted metadata sits at `#766879`.
- **Containers & Elevated Surfaces:** Sub-surfaces use subtle warm tints such as Honey Milk (`#FFF9EE`) and Lavender Foam (`#F7F4FD`).

## Typography

Typography establishes an organic, friendly cadence. 

- **Display & Headlines (Quicksand):** Rounded terminal geometry creates instant kinship with classic illustrated children’s literature and warm storybook lettering. Generous x-height and rounded joints soften structural density.
- **Body & Labels (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Modern, clear, and exceptionally legible across compact screens. Its subtle geometric warmth pairs seamlessly with Quicksand while providing stability and legibility for instructions, narrative copy, and UI controls.
- **Hierarchy Rules:** Reserve Quicksand exclusively for headings and narrative chapter titles. Never set Quicksand below 22px to avoid blurry terminals. All functional indicators, body text, forms, and metadata rely on Plus Jakarta Sans.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy uses a flexible, breathing fluid grid structured around relaxed modularity:

- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `margin-mobile` (1.25rem), `gutter-mobile` (0.75rem). Elements embrace single-column vertical flows with generous vertical spacing to prevent accidental taps.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8 columns, 2rem margins, 1rem gutters. Designed for split storybook/catalog views.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12 columns with a max content container of 1240px, anchored by `margin` (2.5rem) and `gutter` (1.25rem).

Vertical cadence prioritizes comfortable whitespace. Negative space is not treated as an emptiness to fill, but as a soothing pause equivalent to a calm pause in storytelling.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is tactile, plush, and ambient. Harsh, gray, drop-shadow cutouts are strictly avoided in favor of warm, diffuse, honey- and lavender-tinted glows that mimic physical softness:

- **Level 0 (Flat):** Canvas base `#FFFDF9`. Structural content dividers rely on soft borders tinted in 8% Toasted Honey (`rgba(233, 168, 36, 0.08)`) or tinted surfaces rather than heavy lines.
- **Level 1 (Resting Pillows & Cards):** A soft, ambient floor elevation. Shadow: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(142, 125, 190, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(233, 168, 36, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Elements & Floating Panels):** Slightly raised with bouncy responsiveness. Shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(142, 125, 190, 0.12), 0 4px 10px -2px rgba(233, 168, 36, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays & Story Drawers):** High tactile presence. Shadow: `0 16px 36px -6px rgba(61, 50, 62, 0.12), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(142, 125, 190, 0.1)`.
- **Embossed Accent / Squishy Depth:** Active states on primary buttons use an inner bottom shadow (`inset 0 -3px 0 rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.12)`) and an upper micro-highlight (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.4)`) to create the satisfying, physical feel of soft rubber or stuffed fabric.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary is unapologetically pill-shaped, bulbous, and rounded (Level 3). Sharp corners do not exist within this design system; all points are rounded down to resemble smooth wooden blocks, rounded river stones, and plush soft toys.

- **Primary controls, chips, and tags:** Fully pill-shaped (`border-radius: 9999px`).
- **Cards and content blocks:** Large, generous curves (`rounded-lg` at 2rem, `rounded-xl` at 3rem).
- **Interactive feedback:** Ripple rings and focus indicators expand into circular or smooth organic radii with 3px safe-offsets to protect aesthetic softness.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background in Toasted Honey (`#E9A824`), label in Ivory (`#FFFDF9`) or deep plum (`#3D323E`) depending on strict accessibility thresholds, fully pill-shaped. Subtle bottom inset shadow for tactile "squish" on `:active`.
- **Secondary:** Lavender (`#8E7DBE`) background with white label text, or Warm Ivory background with 2px solid Sweet Lavender border.
- **Tertiary / Gentle:** Powder Rose (`#F4ACB7`) tint surface with deep plum text, designed for reassuring actions (e.g., "Remind Me Later", "Save Favorite Lullaby").

### Chips & Badges
- Compact pill-shaped modules. Soft pastel fills: Honey tint (`#FFF4DB`), Lavender tint (`#F1EDFC`), or Rose tint (`#FDEBEF`).
- Text matched to their respective parent primaries at higher contrast for effortless scanning (e.g., `#B0740B`, `#5E4D8F`, `#B85566`).

### Cards & Story Containers
- Enclosed with a continuous 2rem or 3rem radius.
- Background in pure Ivory or ultra-soft Cream (`#FAF6ED`).
- Borderless by default, using Level 1 ambient lavender shadows. For interactive product cards, a 1.5px subtle outline using Powder Rose at 20% opacity (`rgba(244, 172, 183, 0.2)`) guides the eye.

### Input Fields
- Generously padded (`1rem` vertical, `1.25rem` horizontal) with fully pill-shaped forms.
- Backgrounds sit in tinted Ivory with an inactive border of `#EADFCF`.
- On focus: smooth transition into a 2px sweet lavender ring (`#8E7DBE`) with a soft lavender ambient blur. Placeholder text in gentle cocoa `#9E93A2`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Radio controls are dual-circle targets; checkboxes use rounded squares (`border-radius: 0.5rem`).
- Checked states transition smoothly into solid Toasted Honey or Lavender with an ivory rounded checkmark icon. Generous touch targets of at least 48x48px for toddler and parent hands.

### Lists & Navigation
- Section lists feature ample breathing room between list items (`space-md`), separated by rounded pill divider dots or subtle warm borders rather than edge-to-edge rules.
- Floating bottom bar with pill-shaped active navigation states and glowing dot indicators.

### Character & Story Accents
- Dedicated narrative badges, bedtime routine trackers, and soft audio playback scrubbers shaped like rounded organic pills with warm thumb drag handles.
